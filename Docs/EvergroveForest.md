# Evergrove forest

New games wake in `L_EvergroveForest`, a separate, shaded exploration map. Rein finds the player lying in its eastern clearing. Dense groups of the existing Breeze trees form grassy passages, forks and dead ends; there are no dirt roads inside. The layout follows the readability and density of the user's Pokemon forest references without copying their assets.

The expanded route is 1.5 times the original traversal length. The western timber gate transfers the player to `(3200,0)` on the approach path outside the rural village. The return gate leads back to `(2600,600)` in the forest. An in-memory save snapshot carries the exact clock, inventory, quests and player state across transitions. Restoration retries until player systems are ready, with a recursion guard. Legacy town saves inside the previous small rooms migrate to the expanded interiors.

The water bodies remain separate: a curved river at X approximately 5750, lake at `(8500,2500)`, pond at `(4100,2600)`, and pond at `(7950,-4300)`. Tree and groundcover clearance keeps their surfaces visible. The river has a traversable timber bridge. Rocks and fallen logs use original rounded, faceted meshes matching the existing trees.

`forest_water_surface.py` builds two original lit materials inspired by Gamma Emerald's animated-water presentation: a directional river current and slower intersecting lake/pond ripples. Broken crests, moving shallow-edge ripples, depth colors and animated world-space normals avoid flat solid water. Materials respond to the existing day/night lighting rather than emitting light. Reference: [Gamma Emerald surfing showcase](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Ac_bY3Wgu4); no assets or shader code copied.

Author geometry with `build_forest_feature_assets.py`, then schedule `author_forest_level_unreal.py` in the owning editor via `trigger_rural_rebuild.py schedule_forest_level.py`. This builder preserves the expanded town interiors and removes its obsolete inline forest. For material-only updates, use `schedule_smooth_water.py`; enable REIMPORT_WATER only when changing source UVs. Avoid writing a loaded map from another editor process.

Standing water now stays calm at rest. `forest_water_caustics.hlsl` adds local irregular rings only while the player moves in shallow water, driven by `MPC_WaterInteraction` and `BP_WoodlandWorld.UpdateWaterContact`. The river retains directional flow streaks; its channel no longer widens beneath the bridge. No textures from the reference image are imported. `preview_forest_water_motion.py` captures paired frames with the world clock paused for visual inspection in `Saved/WaterMotionAudit`.

Validation: `validate_separate_forest.py` walks the full route and checks bidirectional travel with exact fractional clock continuity; `validate_expanded_rooms_and_forest.py --rooms-only` checks furnished interiors and constant indoor lighting; `validate_opening_sequence.py` checks the prone awakening and standing recovery. Evidence is in `Saved/SeparateForestAudit`, `Saved/ExpandedRoomsAudit`, and `Saved/OpeningSequenceAudit`. The packaged launcher is `Saved/BaselinePackage/Windows/AdventureRPG.exe`.

Riverbank access: `riverbank_thickets.py` fills the former empty riverside clearings with matching bundled trees. Continuous collision closes both banks except at the bridge approach; the main forest route and side passages remain open. `close_forest_riverbanks_unreal.py` applies this without rebuilding the map, and the regular forest builder includes the same placement. `validate_riverbank_closure.py` tests four attempts to leave the bridge along the banks, then walks the main route and checks village travel.

Natural forest pass: `build_canopy_leaves_source.py` creates 1,260 curled, pointed leaves with petioles. `forest_canopy_fall.hlsl` drops them from 740 cm over independent 12?18 second lifetimes, with subtle lateral drift and 3D rocking/yaw. Masked fades hide canopy resets and terrain-aware landings. One world-anchored leaf field and 25 softened wind fields cover the forest; forest effects remain visible at northern coordinates below Y=-5000, while legacy interior hiding remains enabled.

`build_cartoon_logs.py` generates tapered, rounded bark logs with end-grain rings and broken branches. `author_forest_terrain_unreal.py` removes the symmetric shoreline stones and scatters 38 rocks irregularly along forest edges and clearings. `forest_terrain_height.py` defines broad, smooth rises up to about 170 cm; terrain uses complex static collision, and trees and props follow its height. Water margins and the bridge remain level. The matching HLSL height function grounds falling leaves on the same terrain.

Verified natural pass: main-route traversal crosses an actual player-height range of 91?203 cm; all four riverbank-entry attempts remain blocked. Village travel preserves clock and gold. All 26 forest atmosphere actors remain world-anchored at six test locations and visible even at northern Y=-5400. The original wind/interior regression also passes. Evidence: Saved/ForestAtmosphereAudit and Saved/SeparateForestAudit. Backup: Saved/ForestRecoveryBackup/NaturalForestFinished.

Gentle ambient normals create surface movement; irregular rings remain tied to moving player contact. Four sparse, asymmetrically placed lily patches bob and drift by only 1?2 cm. `author_living_ponds_unreal.py` updates standing-water materials and water details without changing the river or rebuilding the forest. Paired lake and pond captures are in Saved/LivingWaterAudit.
