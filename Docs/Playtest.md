## Ten to fifteen minutes

1. Walk around the guild and trail with WASD, including diagonals. Try Shift to
   sprint. Judge speed, stopping, facing and camera distance.
2. Approach Luna, face her and press E. Press E to advance, W/S to select, and E
   to choose a request. Deliver the starter package to Rein with E. J opens and
   closes the quest journal.
3. Meet a beast with E, collect two treats from the small gold cubes, then return
   and press E to befriend it. Repeat until you have three. Use 1/2/3 to switch
   the single exploration follower; switching has a two-second cooldown.
4. Accept a combat or gathering request from Luna and follow the east trail.
   The quest objectives appear in the woodland clearings. Combat should show all
   three companions, both health cards and the action diamond. LMB attacks,
   V switches sword/frost, Q commands the party, and H heals.
5. Return to the guild hub. Combat should stop, the combat panels should hide,
   and only your selected follower should remain. Watch for companions snagging
   on fences, rocks or trees, and note the location if they do.
6. With dialogue and the journal closed, press F5 to save. Move somewhere else,
   then press F9 to reload. Check your position, quests and selected companion.

## Useful feedback

- Movement: too slow, about right, or too fast? Does diagonal facing feel natural?
- Camera: too close, about right, or too far? Does scenery hide the player?
- Combat: can you read HP and find the next action quickly? Are hits clear?
- Companions: where did one get stuck or fall behind?
- Overall: which part felt enjoyable, and which part felt confusing?

The trees, guild shell, beasts and trail are blockout assets. The latest build
adds companion pathfinding, enemy return behavior, attack-radius markers and
damage numbers. Watch followers route around the hub fence, trees and rocks;
try leaving an enemy's clearing and dodging out of its gold warning disk.
Finished environment art follows the layout and camera decisions.
