# Woodland clock, day/night cycle, and 3D lanterns

Added 2026-10-15. The five outdoor sprite lanterns are now solid 2.76 m posts
with stepped bases, brass collars, framed warm glass and pitched roof caps.
Their iron, brass and roof materials match the guild exterior. Source geometry
is generated by `Scripts/build_3d_lantern.py` in `ContentSource/Lantern3D`.
`Scripts/import_3d_lantern.py` imports the mesh and updates runtime dressing.
The lantern meshes retain no collision or navigation blocking.

The five lanterns now run farther down the trail at X=900, 1600, 2300, 3000,
and 3700 cm, all at Y=-220 cm. `woodland_lighting_settings.py` is the shared
placement source for the mesh dressing and actual lights.

The camera is now pitched at -31 degrees indoors and outdoors (previously
-34), retaining orthographic width 1200. Its follow offset is 2496 cm at height
1500 cm. Indoors it follows player X/Y without the old room-edge clamps and
continues following height on the upper floor, using the same smoothing as
outdoors. `polish_lantern_light_and_camera.py` applies these changes.

Lantern glass is translucent so the real point source can shine through it.
The amber glow is reduced to a maximum strength of 4, with a 6 cm source radius
for softer light. Lantern point lights use 300 lumens and a 550 cm falloff radius;
window lights use 350 lumens and a 240 cm radius. Night lighting remains readable
without those sources: moon intensity is 2.0, sky fill is 1.0 with a cool night
tint, and unlit art
retains 30 percent of its daytime brightness. Lanterns serve as route markers.
Their light is warm white, with shadows disabled on the local sources to avoid
harsh silhouettes from the lantern housing. Sun/moon shadows remain.

`BP_WoodlandWorld` owns the clock and advances it using gameplay DeltaSeconds.
It starts on day 1 at 08:00. Each full day lasts 1200 real gameplay seconds
(20 minutes); 10 minutes of daylight and 10 minutes of night. Dawn begins at
06:00 and dusk reaches night at 18:00. The clock keeps running inside the guild.

Editable fields on the placed Woodland_World actor:

- `TimeOfDayMinutes`: minutes since midnight, normally 0–1439.999.
- `DayNumber`: current day, starting at 1.
- `RealSecondsPerDay`: cycle length; values below 1 are clamped to 1 internally.
- `ClockPaused`: freezes clock progression; lighting still reflects the set time.

Readout fields for future UI/gameplay: `ClockHour`, `ClockMinute`, `DayProgress`
(0–1), `IsNight`, and `Daylight` (0–1). Midnight increments DayNumber and wraps
time, including steps spanning multiple days. Gameplay pause/time dilation also
affects the clock through Unreal's DeltaSeconds.

The cycle changes sun/moon direction, light color, directional intensity and sky
fill. Five lantern point lights and ten exterior window point lights illuminate
nearby surfaces at night. The shared
`MPC_WorldClock.NightBrightness` parameter dims stylized unlit environment art
without replacing its textures, palette or wind animation. Default collection
brightness is 1 so the editor retains its normal daytime appearance.

`NightLightFactor` controls exterior light output separately from daylight:
it ramps from 0 to 1 between 16:00 and 19:00, stays fully on overnight, and
fades from 1 to 0 between 05:00 and 07:00. From 07:00 to 16:00, both actual
light intensity and lantern/window material emission are zero. Window and
lantern glass reference `MPC_WorldClock.NightGlow` for matching progressive glow.

The five procedural outdoor planters have been removed from the runtime
dressing and from the scenery authoring source.

Stable interior lighting uses hidden, noncolliding box meshes tagged
`StableInteriorLighting`. The guild has `GuildInterior_StableLightingZone`,
covering both floors. When the player is inside a tagged box, scene lighting
and unlit artwork use fixed daytime values while the game clock continues.
Exterior night lighting returns on exit. This is intended for the current
single-player game and applies to the active view.

For future buildings, duplicate this zone, resize its cube to enclose the
interior, keep the `StableInteriorLighting` tag, and keep visibility, collision,
shadows and navigation influence disabled. Zone bounds are world-aligned;
use an unrotated box. Buildings in separate maps need the same world controller
or an equivalent clock/lighting setup.

New play sessions start on day 1 at 08:00. F5/F9 quick-save/load now also persists
the exact day, fractional-minute clock, cycle duration and pause state. Loading
refreshes lighting immediately and resumes from the saved time, including after
a restart; offline wall-clock time is not added. Older saves have no recorded
clock and leave the current time unchanged. See [SaveLoad.md](SaveLoad.md).

Authoring: run `connect_night_materials.py`, `finish_day_night_lighting.py`,
then `author_day_night.py` with the
woods loaded in the connected editor. The lantern geometry/import scripts run
before those when rebuilding the assets from source. The authoring operation
initializes the placed world's clock defaults to match its Blueprint defaults.
After source asset imports, run `move_trail_lanterns.py` and
`polish_lantern_light_and_camera.py` before `author_day_night.py` to retain the
trail placements, translucent glass and latest camera refinement.

Validation: `validate_day_night.py` verifies clock progression, pause, midnight
rollover, five solid lanterns and fifteen point lights, collision settings,
removed planters, progressive morning/evening fades, zero daytime emission,
and identical guild interior light settings at noon and midnight. It captures
actual PIE game frames outdoors and indoors using the temporary existing guild
movement probe.
The probe is removed afterward. Results are in `Saved/DayNightAudit/runtime.json`.
`validate_world_anchored_wind.py` also passed after the lighting changes.
The current validator also tests unclamped indoor following in X/Y and height,
and captures night views with `ExteriorLightScale` at 1 and 0. The separate
`measure_lantern_light.ps1` check measures actual illumination of the path in
those captures, keeping visible material glow unchanged.
It also verifies that forest artwork darkens compared with noon and that the
path remains readable with actual point lights disabled. Coverage includes
grass materials with a separate movement collection, plus woodland and cozy
materials under `Game/Combat`, as well as the environment folder.
